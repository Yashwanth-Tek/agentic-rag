Which departments were above the readmission target in August 2026?

✓ Verified3 tool calls27.1 seconds3 sourcesRevised once
Analyzed→ Search readmission rate by department August 2026→ Search readmission target→ Listed 7 documents→ Reviewed→ Validated→ Answer

Cardiology and Oncology were above the readmission target in August 2026: the 30-day readmission target for the third quarter of 2026 is 11.5 percent for every department, and a department counts as above target when its monthly rate is higher than 11.5 percent, while Cardiology recorded 12.1 percent in August 2026 and Oncology recorded 11.6 percent in August 2026.12

The board minutes record that Cardiology had been above the 30-day readmission target in July and August, and that the department had been asked for an improvement plan.3

The other two departments were below the 11.5 percent target that month: Orthopedics recorded 8.3 percent in August 2026 and Neurology recorded 10.4 percent in August 2026.2
