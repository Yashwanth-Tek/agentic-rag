# Lakeside Health Partners — Visitor Handbook

This handbook explains what visitors can expect at Lakeside Health Partners hospitals. It is a
fictional sample document.

## Visiting hours

General wards welcome visitors from 11:00 to 20:00 every day. Intensive care units allow two
visitors at a time between 14:00 and 19:00. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult.

## Parking

The visitor car park is open 24 hours. Parking costs 4 dollars per hour, capped at 18 dollars per
day. Patients attending chemotherapy or dialysis receive free parking on presentation of their
appointment letter at the main reception.

## Accessibility

Every entrance has step-free access. Wheelchairs can be borrowed from the main reception desk
against a refundable deposit of 20 dollars. Hearing loops are installed in all outpatient waiting
areas.

## Food services

The Lakeside Café on the ground floor is open from 07:00 to 19:00 on weekdays and from 09:00 to
16:00 at weekends. Vending machines on every ward floor are available at all times.

## Contact

Visitor enquiries can be made at the main reception or by calling the switchboard on
555-0100 (fictional number).
