Lakeside Health Partners
Board of Directors - Minutes of the meeting held on 24 September 2026 (fictional)

Present: the Chair, the Chief Executive, the Chief Medical Officer, the Chief Financial Officer and five non-executive directors. Apologies were received from one non-executive director.

1. Minutes of the previous meeting
The minutes of the meeting held on 27 August 2026 were approved without amendment.

2. Quality report
The Chief Medical Officer presented the quarterly quality report. The board noted that Cardiology had been above the 30-day readmission target in July and August and that an improvement plan had been requested from the department. The board asked for a progress update at the November meeting.

3. Capital investment
The board approved the purchase of a second MRI scanner for the Radiology department at a cost of 2.4 million dollars. Delivery is expected in November 2026 and the scanner is planned to be in service by January 2027. The Chief Financial Officer confirmed that the purchase is funded from the 2026 capital budget.

4. Visitor parking
Following complaints about parking charges, the board agreed to keep the current tariff of 4 dollars per hour but to extend free parking to patients attending dialysis, in addition to those attending chemotherapy. The change takes effect on 1 October 2026.

5. Workforce
The Chief Executive reported that nursing vacancies had fallen to 6 percent, down from 9 percent in March 2026. The board congratulated the recruitment team.

6. Any other business
There was no other business. The next meeting will be held on 29 October 2026.
